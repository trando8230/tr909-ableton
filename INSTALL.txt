TR-909 Rhythm Composer for Ableton Live
Apple silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4). This is a real VST3 and Audio Unit, not a Max device.

Install:
  1. Quit Live.
  2. Copy TR-909.vst3 into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
     Create that folder if it is missing.
  3. Copy TR-909.component into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  4. If macOS says the plugin is damaged, open Terminal and run:
       xattr -cr ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/TR-909.vst3
       xattr -cr ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/TR-909.component
  5. Open Live. Preferences, Plug-Ins, rescan.
  6. On a MIDI track choose TR-909 under VST3 or Audio Units.
     The window floats over the arrangement like any other instrument.

Press Play in Live. It follows Live's tempo.
Click a pad to cycle off, hit, accent.
